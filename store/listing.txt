RightSave

Save images and direct-file videos with right-click, large previews, and social-site media detection.

Free: unlimited individual Save As, large previews, Load all, search, site controls, media detection, canvas/SVG and video-frame export.

Pro: ZIP collections, saved workflows, batch Save As, custom filenames, dimension filters and list export. Pro sales are not open yet. No billing is active.

Protected or segmented streams cannot be assembled. Reddit video-only tracks are labeled. Some sites require login and signed URLs can expire.
