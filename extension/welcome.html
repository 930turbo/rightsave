Quick start

Ready to save.

Pin RightSave in your browser’s Extensions menu. Open a webpage, then right-click an image or supported video to choose a filename and folder.

Your first save

  1. Refresh webpages that were open before installing.
  2. Open the post or image you want. Play a video first if its file is hidden behind a player.
  3. Right-click the media for Save As, or click RightSave in the toolbar and choose Open media library.
  4. In the library, choose Load all previews. Rescan after scrolling or opening a carousel to discover newly loaded media.

Social websites

Enhanced detection supports media-data patterns used by Instagram, Facebook, X, Reddit, Threads, and LinkedIn. It reads media URLs from content your browser has already loaded. Use the site normally, including signing in when required. Nothing is uploaded to RightSave.

Direct MP4/WebM files can be saved. Protected playback and segmented HLS/DASH streams cannot be combined in this build. Reddit’s separate video track is clearly marked Video only. Site changes can affect compatibility.

Make it yours

Turn off Right-click Save As to use the browser’s RightSave submenu. Hold Shift while right-clicking for the normal browser menu, or Alt for website controls. Alt + Shift + S opens the library.
