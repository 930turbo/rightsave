How buying will work

Checkout opens at Lemon Squeezy in a browser tab. Your receipt includes a license key; paste it below to activate your paid tools. Card details stay with the payment provider.

A browser profile uses one activation. Deactivate it here before moving to another profile. Paid tools check the license when used, usually once a day, with up to seven days offline after a successful check. Free saving always works without a license.

Prices are a proposal for launch. Both paid plans are one-time purchases with no automatic renewal. Final prices and taxes will appear at checkout when sales open.
