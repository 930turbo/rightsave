Saving a file

Right-click the media and choose its filename and folder. Turn off direct right-click saving in Settings to use the browser’s RightSave submenu.

Shift + right-click opens the usual browser menu. Alt + right-click gives the website control.

Finding more

Open a post, scroll its carousel or play its video, then rescan. Use format, shape and width filters to narrow the gallery. Click a loaded image to browse a larger preview with the arrow keys.

A preview will not load

The media host may require you to be signed in or may block previews. Keep the post open and try saving the original. Load all previews never starts downloads.

Videos and temporary files

Direct MP4 and WebM files can be saved. Protected playback and segmented streams are not combined. Video-only tracks are labeled. Firefox’s .part file is temporary; wait for Downloads to finish.
